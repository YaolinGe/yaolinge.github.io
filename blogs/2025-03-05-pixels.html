Pixelated images

Pixelated image

This image shows the head of a seagull and a zoomed-in section, revealing the pixel structure. Each pixel is a small square of color, typically defined by red, green, and blue (RGB) values. Pixelation occurs when an image is enlarged beyond its resolution, making individual pixels visible.

What is pixelation?
Pixelation is the display of individual pixels, usually due to low resolution or high magnification.

Why is it useful?
Understanding pixels helps in image editing, compression, and digital art. It allows for precise color manipulation and optimization for web or print.

How to inspect pixels?
Use image editing tools (like Photoshop, GIMP, or browser zoom) to magnify images and observe pixel patterns and color values.
